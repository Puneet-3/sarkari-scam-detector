ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLES

Recognize Common Scam Patterns

These examples are educational, not live PIB advisories. Verify a scheme through independently found official contact details before sharing information or paying a fee.

FAKE SCHEME Example 1

PM Fasal Bima Yojana Processing Fee Claim

The Claim: A WhatsApp message offering a compensation payout of ₹2,50,000 under the PM Fasal Bima Yojana, asking for ₹2,500 registration fee to process it.

What to check: An unsolicited payout paired with an advance fee is a warning sign. Find the scheme independently and confirm eligibility, fees, and the payment process before acting.
FAKE NAUKRI Example 2

Digital India Recruitment Cell Vacancies

The Claim: SMS claiming 500+ job vacancies in Digital India Cell without interview. Requires application fee payment on unofficial domains like `pm-yojana-verify.co`.

What to check: Locate the recruiting organization's published vacancy notice independently. Compare the application address, fee, and selection process before sending money or documents.
FAKE LOTTERY Example 3

Sarkari / KBC Lucky Draw Bonus

The Claim: WhatsApp message claiming you have won a Sarkari lucky draw of ₹5,00,000. Urges clicking a link and sending details to claim.

What to check: An unexpected prize asking for payment or sensitive information deserves independent verification. Do not share an OTP or banking credentials to claim a prize.
ADVISORY Example 4

How to spot a Fake Government website?

Fraudsters clone layouts of official pages (e.g. PM Kisan, Skill India) and host them on extensions like `.xyz`, `.co`, `.info`, `.online`, `.org.in`.

What to check: Inspect the full hostname. scheme.gov.in.example.com belongs to example.com. A government-style domain or familiar logo alone does not verify a message; find the service independently.
